# Portrait

Drop your hero portrait here, commit it, and pick it in **Admin → Profile → Portrait**.

- **4:5 crop** fits the frame exactly. Anything else is cover-cropped from the
  centre, so keep your face away from the edges.
- The photo renders as an **ember duotone** that resolves to full colour on
  hover, so pick a shot with real separation between lights and darks. A flat,
  evenly-lit frame maps to a narrow band of orange and loses its shape.
- Around 1200×1500 is plenty — `next/image` generates the smaller sizes.

Leave this folder empty and the frame shows a designed initials plate instead;
nothing breaks.
